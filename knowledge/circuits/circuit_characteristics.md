# F1 Circuit Characteristics

## High-Speed Circuits

### Monza (Italian Grand Prix)
- **Type**: Permanent, high-speed, low downforce
- **Laps**: 53
- **Key features**: Long straights (Rettifilo, Curva Grande straight),
  chicanes, low-downforce setup
- **Tire demand**: Low lateral stress, high longitudinal stress
- **Typical strategy**: 1-stop (MEDIUM -> HARD)
- **DRS zones**: 2 (main straight, back straight)
- **Overtaking**: Common on main straight into T1

### Silverstone (British Grand Prix)
- **Type**: Permanent, high-speed, high downforce
- **Laps**: 52
- **Key features**: Fast flowing corners (Maggots-Becketts-Chapel),
  high-speed Copse, Stowe hairpin
- **Tire demand**: Very high lateral load, especially on front-left
- **Typical strategy**: 1-2 stops depending on degradation
- **DRS zones**: 2

### Suzuka (Japanese Grand Prix)
- **Type**: Permanent, figure-8 layout, high downforce
- **Laps**: 53
- **Key features**: Esses, 130R, Degner curves, Spoon curve
- **Tire demand**: Very high, both lateral and longitudinal
- **Typical strategy**: 1-2 stops
- **DRS zones**: 2

## Street Circuits

### Monaco (Monaco Grand Prix)
- **Type**: Street circuit, ultra-low speed
- **Laps**: 78
- **Key features**: Tunnel, Swimming Pool, Rascasse, narrow width
- **Tire demand**: Low degradation, track position critical
- **Typical strategy**: 1-stop as late as possible
- **DRS zones**: 1
- **Overtaking**: Extremely difficult, undercut is primary tool

### Jeddah (Saudi Arabian Grand Prix)
- **Type**: Street circuit, high-speed
- **Laps**: 50
- **Key features**: 27 corners, 6.1km length, walls close to track
- **Tire demand**: Moderate, but wall proximity makes wear critical
- **Typical strategy**: 1-2 stops
- **Safety Car probability**: High (~45%)

## Tilke Circuits

### Bahrain (Bahrain Grand Prix)
- **Type**: Permanent, Tilke design, medium-speed
- **Laps**: 57
- **Key features**: Four long straights, heavy braking zones,
  abrasive surface
- **Tire demand**: Very high, rear-limited
- **Typical strategy**: 1-2 stops (often 2 due to high degradation)
- **DRS zones**: 3

### Shanghai (Chinese Grand Prix)
- **Type**: Permanent, Tilke design
- **Laps**: 56
- **Key features**: Long back straight, flowing section, hairpin
- **Tire demand**: Moderate-high, front-limited
- **Typical strategy**: 1-2 stops
- **DRS zones**: 2

## Sprint Race Circuits (2025)
- Chinese Grand Prix (R02)
- Miami Grand Prix (R06)
- Austrian Grand Prix (R11)
- Sprint races: 1/3 race distance, no mandatory tire change, separate
